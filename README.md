# Perfectly Realistic Investment Banking Simulator™
You are hired by companies (well just nvidia because time constraints) to help them "bounce back" from their plummeting stock prices.
you climb on on a randomly generated platforms, trying to reach the original stock price, dragging the line representing the stock price up with you

## controls
once you get into the main menu, all you have to do is to left click the "nvidia" button on the left, and then the gameplay is one key. you can 
either press space or left click to jump, and your goal is to climb higher on the platforms. gold coins give you a boost up!
